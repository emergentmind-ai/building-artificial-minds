# Books

**Series:**

### Building Artificial Minds
***The Principles of Creating Intelligence***

Four perspectives on emergence, one common framework: This series explores how intelligence emerges.

Each volume approaches a diffserent aspect of the question of how intelligence emerges—and how it might be artificially created:


#### Volume 1: Evolutionary Theories for AI

*How do new capabilities emerge?*

Evolution is the only known process that has demonstrably produced intelligent systems. This volume examines which principles of evolution can be transferred to artificial systems and how learning, adaptation, and creativity can emerge from evolutionary mechanisms.

[About the Book]({{ '/en/books/evolutionary-theories-for-ai/' | relative_url }})


#### Volume 2: Swarm Intelligence for AI

*How does collective intelligence emerge?*

This volume explores how many simple units can generate complex behavior through communication and interaction. Swarms demonstrate how intelligence can emerge without centralized control.


#### Volume 3: Complex Adaptive Systems for AI

*How do complex structures remain stable?*

This volume explores self-organization, dynamics, and emergence in adaptive systems. It examines how stable structures can emerge even as their individual components are constantly changing.


#### Volume 4: Information Theory for AI

*What is actually being learned?*

This volume explores the role of information in intelligent systems. It examines how information is stored, processed, and used to build knowledge and internal models of the world.


#### Volume 5: The Architecture of Intelligence

*How can these principles be combined into an intelligent system?*

The final volume brings together the principles developed throughout the series into an architecture for artificial intelligence. It describes how a system could be designed that does not merely solve tasks, but can learn autonomously, adapt to changing conditions, and develop new capabilities.
