# Building Artificial Minds
## The Principles of Creating Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is one of the most ambitious technological endeavors of our time. Ever larger models are trained on ever more data, while increasingly powerful computers process ever more complex patterns. The progress is impressive. Yet one fundamental question remains open:

**Do we already understand how intelligence emerges—or are we merely building systems that reproduce individual aspects of intelligent behavior?**
